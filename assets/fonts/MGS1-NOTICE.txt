These are recreations of bitmap interface glyphs seen in Metal Gear Solid (Konami, 1998),
not a universal PlayStation system font and not a Resident Evil font.

Local derivatives, 2026:
- mgs1-codec-fr.ttf (MGS1 Codec FR): added French accented pixel glyphs, OE ligatures,
  typographic punctuation aliases and adjusted vertical metrics. Base Latin outlines preserved.
- mgs1-ammo-hud.ttf (MGS1 Ammo HUD): remaps existing optional five-pixel-wide ammo
  glyphs to the ordinary digits 0-9. Source font glyph outlines are unchanged.
